## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router, TypeScript), chosen by the client. Static generation, hosting not decided yet. Styling, animation and content tooling delegated: Tailwind v4, GSAP (single animation library), content kept in typed files under `content/` so the yearly programme can change without touching the design.

## Users

- **Regional families and visitors** (Saguenay–Lac-Saint-Jean, 20 minutes north of Chicoutimi) deciding whether to come, when, and what there is to do. Most arrive from Facebook on a phone.
- **Gymkhana competitors** (around 100 per edition) checking the competition schedule.
- **Country music and line-dance fans** looking for the evening shows.
- **Partners** (72 listed) who fund a free event and expect visibility.

## Product Purpose

The website of the Festival Gymkhana de Saint-David-de-Falardeau. Its first job is to inform visitors about the festival and show the programme. Success: a visitor understands within seconds what the festival is, when and where it happens, that entry is free, and finds the programme.

## Positioning

A four-day western festival built around a real equestrian sport: gymkhana (timed barrel racing, rescue race, slalom, relay, flag race) sanctioned by the CBSLC under Cheval Québec rules. Competition by day, country shows and dancing at night, family activities all weekend, free entry. Organised since 2007 by a volunteer committee whose motto is to welcome the public and competitors like family.

## Operating Context

- Site: Place des Festivals, 51, rue du Chalet, Saint-David-de-Falardeau (Québec) G0V 1C0. Sand competition arena with stands, show tent with bar, chalet des loisirs (breakfasts), family zone, temporary camping, parking.
- Usually Thursday to Sunday, second weekend of August. Evening highlight: Grand Prix barils et sauvetage, Saturday 19 h.
- The previous WordPress site (festivalfalardeau.com) is gone: the domain was lost in December 2025 and now belongs to an unrelated third party. Nothing to migrate; a new domain is needed.
- Facebook page: https://www.facebook.com/FGF2015

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Language: French only.
- Entry is free. Camping 40 $ for the weekend (2024 and 2025 rate), no reservation, first come first served.
- Site rules: no alcohol or glass brought on site; dogs on leash at all times and not allowed in the stands.
- Contacts: 418-540-5700 (information and camping), 418-820-6134 (kiosks and exhibitors), festivalfalardeau@gmail.com.
- Undecided: 2027 dates and edition number, 2027 programme, hosting, final domain. Until the 2027 programme exists the site may show the 2024 programme, always labelled as a preview of a previous edition.

## Brand Commitments

- Name shown in full: Festival Gymkhana de Saint-David-de-Falardeau (official short form in archives: Festival Gymkhana de Falardeau).
- Logo: gold sheriff star with barrel racer, final version carrying www.festivalfalardeau.ca (`photo_clients/banque_photos/logo-festival-final-ca.png`, built by `npm run images -- logo`).

- Partner tiers are named after gaits: Au grand galop, Au galop, Au trot, Au pas.
- Client direction: strong country and western identity, warm sand colours, festival atmosphere, as premium and high-end as possible.

## Product Principles

1. The sport is the hero: what no other western festival can copy is the gymkhana itself.
2. Information first: dates, place, free entry and the programme are never more than one move away.
3. Family welcome: the tone is warm and local, never exclusive or cold, however premium the finish.
4. Real over invented: real names, real rules, real history; placeholders are labelled.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Audience spans children to seniors, mostly on phones outdoors: high contrast, large touch targets, reduced motion respected, all content readable without animation.
