# Home page (landing)

## Scope
Single landing page at `/`, French. Visitor mode: Persuade, with Experience in the hero and the gymkhana section.

## Surface strategy
- Audience: regional families first, then competitors, country fans and partners. Mostly phone, arriving from Facebook.
- Job: understand the festival in seconds and reach the programme.
- Primary action: Voir la programmation (in-page programme with day tabs). Secondary: Planifier ma visite (address, camping, rules).
- Proof: real photos, real rules and events, history since 2007, 60 volunteers, partner tiers.
- Constraints: 2027 dates and programme unknown (show « Août 2027 » and the 2024 programme labelled as a preview). Partner logos missing (names only). Several photos watermarked.
- Section order: hero, le festival en bref, programmation, le gymkhana, activités famille, planifier ma visite, l'équipe, partenaires, pied de page.
- Memorable moment: GYMKHANA set huge across the hero with the rider passing in front of the letters; the cloverleaf barrel pattern that runs from « Départ » under the hero to « Arrivée » in the footer.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The sport is the spectacle. The page is built on the gymkhana itself (barrels, cloverleaf, chrono, dust) and refuses the category default of boot, skull and guitar patterns in orange and brown, popups, and a centred title over a stock photo.
OWN-WORLD: Sand #E6CFA3, dust #B98D58, earth #6B4429, night #141619, one accent barrel red #C4381F reserved for action; sage and pale sky as quiet nuance. One variable family, Archivo: ultra-wide black for display, condensed for times and labels. Square corners everywhere, 1px rules, rectangular buttons, fine photographic grain, warm slightly desaturated photography.
STORY: The visitor feels the speed of a barrel turn, reads the full festival name, date, place and free entry, opens the programme, learns what gymkhana is, sees what the family can do, knows where to go and where to camp, meets the volunteers, sees the partners.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed golden-hour barrel photo. Bottom: three-line lockup FESTIVAL / GYMKHANA / DE SAINT-DAVID-DE-FALARDEAU, GYMKHANA spanning the width, the rider cut-out layered in front of the letters. Above the lockup, left: one-sentence pitch and the red « Voir la programmation » button; right: « Août 2027 » and « Entrée gratuite ». Thin transparent nav on top: logo, Programmation, Le gymkhana, Activités, « Planifier ma visite ».
FORM: User-pinned direction « L'Arène » (direction 1 of 3 presented, chosen by the client, with day-to-night programme from « Grand Chapiteau » and family warmth from « Terre de Falardeau »). No concept-seed key: direction pinned by the client. Build path: code-led. Signature interaction: letters stretching from condensed to wide on entry (the start of a run), three-plane hero parallax, scroll-drawn cloverleaf, one day-to-night colour shift in the programme.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
